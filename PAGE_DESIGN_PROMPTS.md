# Kentron interior-page prompts

Use this frame for every route: preserve the finished home screen’s warm graphite one-dark-world palette, apricot as the only accent, Archivo typography, floating dock, sparse hairlines, and map-native tone. Refuse gradients, glass, glow, colored icon tiles, dashboard cards, badges, giant marketing copy, and the repeated `PageHead + stacked divider list` template.

## Home map — Category Signal

The filter is a single bottom-left button carrying the layers icon; it opens a short list where each category is shown or hidden, and the button counts the active ones. There is no top Categories dropdown and no permanent legend bar. Six categories cover the city without becoming a directory: Technology & learning, Arts & culture, Music & nightlife, Food & drink, Markets & community, and Outdoors & movement. Markers are one language: a compact single-piece locator pin with category color and a reduced Lucide pictogram. Live and planned pins use the same artwork; only live pins sit on a soft animated ground glow. Zoomed-out count clusters keep the apricot ring in both appearances, never black-and-orange in light mode. The right-hand list does not fade individual rows; a soft bottom mask lets the list disappear into the panel. Its collapsed desktop state is a slim vertical edge tab with result count, not a floating pill. Distances appear only after the person grants location, measured from their position. The timeline stays at the top; locate sits in the bottom-right gutter above the zoom controls and beside the results panel. Popup close is a centered drawn X inside a visible circle. Live detail replaces “Still here” with Directions and Open on map. Appearance is System / Dark / Light. The app still opens on the map.

## Search — City Aperture

Design one focused search instrument opening into results. A featured result anchors the left; a day-grouped result ledger sits on the right. Search, timeline, and count share one baseline. Use unboxed rows and local illustration stamps.

## Create — Contribution Desk

Design a task-selection screen, not two equal feature cards. Use one asymmetric media field and a decision column titled “What are you adding?” Two broad stacked action rows distinguish “Happening now” from “Planned for later.”

## Saved — Week Fold

Design a personal pocket agenda. Active saved live posts form one short band at the top and disappear at expiry; planned saves align to day positions beneath. Empty days remain visible and quiet. Both types use image, status or time, title, place, and a remove control.

## Content detail — City Ticket

Planned events and live posts use one wide slip: a full-width 16:7 local image above an asymmetric information band. Put status, title, context, and optional organiser on the left; place and the consistent Save, Directions, Open on map, Report sequence on the right. Only status language and type-specific facts differ. Add one shallow nearby strip.

## Settings — Control Room

Design one operational control board. A narrow browser/device summary sits left; appearance (System / Dark / Light), map, activity, and motion controls occupy one broad surface on the right, separated by spacing and single rules rather than cards.

## Activity — Signal Log

Avoid a notification-center template. Use a quiet summary field beside a chronological log with one thin spine. Saved events use an outlined bookmark; live entries use an apricot point and a small image.

## Profile — Organiser Passport

Use an identity rail for the signed-in account and a quiet publishing summary. Creating, saving, reporting, and managing content require an account; browsing and public settings do not.

## Organiser — Publishing Desk

Design one quiet publishing list, not a dashboard. Use the normal page heading and a single panel of image-led rows grouped under Published, Unpublished, and Weekly templates. Live and planned content share one row anatomy. Keep account navigation below the panel; use no KPI cards, totals rail, or charts.

## About — City Field Note

Use one tall Yerevan illustration beside an edited field note. Give “Right now” and “This week” dominant numbered sections, then one compact strip for privacy, sources, and contact.
