# Production content checklist

Kentron must launch with real, permissioned content. Do not scrape, copy, or ingest events from other websites.

## Planned events

- Confirm the organiser has supplied or approved the title, description, venue, date, time, image, and organiser attribution.
- Confirm the organiser owns or is permitted to publish the image.
- Confirm the event is in Yerevan and the map pin matches the venue.
- Confirm the date is current and expressed in Yerevan time.
- Confirm the category is one of Kentron’s six categories.
- Confirm links or ticket instructions included in the description are accurate.
- Publish from the organiser’s own account where possible.
- Recheck every launch event on the day before launch; unpublish cancelled or uncertain events.

## Live posts

- Never pre-populate live posts as launch fixtures.
- A live post must be created by a person currently observing the scene.
- Do not include faces without permission, private information, exact home addresses, or unsafe activity.
- Verify the public pin is coarse and the post expires after four hours.

## Launch-day minimum

- Use organiser-supplied events only; an empty area is preferable to invented content.
- Aim for useful geographic and category coverage, but do not create filler to meet a number.
- Keep a record outside Kentron of the organiser’s permission and a contact for corrections.
- Assign one staff account to review reports and one person to verify event status.

## Final checks

- No seed or fixture IDs appear in the production database.
- No event was copied from another listing service.
- Every event image has permission and meaningful alt context is represented by its title/description.
- Expired or cancelled events are unpublished.
- `hello@kentron.am` is monitored for correction, privacy, and deletion requests.
