## Direction contract

THESIS: Kentron is a blue-hour city instrument, not a SaaS dashboard. The map leads and chrome recedes.

OWN-WORLD: Basalt ground, painted-metal raised surfaces, warm paper ink, dust-muted text, and one oxidized-copper accent. Condensed civic display type meets a quiet workhorse sans.

STORY: A person sees what is live or planned, understands distance and time, opens a pin, and decides whether to walk.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Collapsible city rail, full-height map, compact time control, location at top-right, and a quiet sibling results index. Mobile replaces rails with tabs and a three-state sheet.

FORM: Operate-mode coordinated map/list split, chosen from the user-approved replacement prompt; no concept roll required because the visual world was explicitly locked.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
